About Us

A Journey Across Three Countries

Maze Kusina is more than just food and goods—it's a story of culture, love, and flavor that spans continents.

Born in the Philippines and raised in Japan from age 12, our founder brings together the warmth of Filipino cooking with the refined elegance of Japanese cuisine. High school classmates in the Philippines who never imagined they'd reunite, fate brought them together again—this time in National City, California, with two beautiful sons and a shared dream.

Today, she shares her culinary talents and eye for beautiful things with the community, creating homemade meals that comfort the soul, baked goods that bring smiles, and curating unique items from San Diego's local treasures.

Every dish, every item tells a story. And now, we're honored to share it with you.
